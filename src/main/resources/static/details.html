Elephants

Elephants are the largest land animals on Earth. They are known for their intelligence, strong social bonds, and long memories. Found primarily in Africa and Asia, these gentle giants are herbivores and can consume up to 300 pounds of food in a single day.

Elephants play a crucial role in maintaining ecological balance by shaping their environments. Unfortunately, they are endangered due to poaching and habitat loss.
